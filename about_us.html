// Our Practice

We travel slowly, and we write what we actually find.

Roamly started in 2015 as a shared travel journal between three friends who were tired of generic travel lists. We wanted something closer to an archive — a place where every entry had been visited, photographed and considered before publication.

A decade later that principle hasn't changed. We still travel to every place we recommend. We still write every entry ourselves. And we still publish only what we'd send to a friend.
