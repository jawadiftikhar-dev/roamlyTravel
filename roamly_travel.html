// Foreword

We document places the way an archivist documents artifacts — with patience, and with care.

Roamly isn't a booking engine. It's a visual archive — a collection of destinations observed over a decade of travel. Every entry is reviewed by hand, photographed with intention, and published only when it feels genuinely worth your time.

We think travel advice should read like a letter from a well-traveled friend, not a sponsored list. The archive reflects that — one place at a time, documented with restraint.

— The Roamly Editorial Team Copenhagen · Lisbon
